# Technical Handoff: "The Clinical Sanctuary" POS System UI-to-Code Prompt

## 1. Project Overview
The goal is to convert a high-fidelity design for a premium body care Point of Sale (POS) system into a functional, responsive web application. The design follows a "Clinical Sanctuary" aesthetic—clean, professional, and calming.

## 2. Design System & Style Guide
The application must adhere to the **Serene Slate POS** design system:
- **Typography:** Primary font is **Manrope**. Use Inter as a secondary body font for high-density data.
- **Color Palette:**
    - Primary Action: `#2D7D78` (Deep Teal)
    - Backgrounds: Light grays (`#F2F4F6`, `#E6E8EA`) and White.
    - Accents: Tonal variants of teal and slate.
- **Roundness:** `ROUND_EIGHT` (8px border-radius) for most containers and buttons.
- **Layout:** Fixed vertical side navigation (full height) with a horizontal top navigation bar.

## 3. Screen Breakdown & Functional Requirements

### A. POS Selling Interface ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_15}})
- **Grid Layout:** Display services (Sauna, Shower) and products (Shampoo, Soap, etc.) in a touch-friendly card grid.
- **Persistence:** The 'Active Cart' sidebar must be persistent and update in real-time.
- **Interactions:** Clicking a card adds an item to the cart. Cart should allow quantity adjustments and removal.

### B. Product & Service Management ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_14}})
- **Data Table:** Implement a sortable table for inventory.
- **Profit Tracking:** Display 'Wholesale' vs. 'Retail' price with an auto-calculated 'Profit Margin' percentage.
- **CRUD Operations:** "Add New Item" button should trigger a form; individual rows should have 'Edit' and 'Delete' actions.

### C. Financial Stats Dashboard ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_7}})
- **Metrics:** Display high-level KPIs: Total Revenue, Total Expenses, and Net Profit.
- **Visualizations:** Include a bar chart for "Monthly Profit vs. Expenses" and progress bars for inventory efficiency.
- **Recent Activity:** A feed of real-time ledger updates.

### D. Users & Roles Management ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_13}})
- **Staff Directory:** A list view of team members with their assigned roles and status.
- **Permissions:** Include a "Role Insight" popover/tooltip that explains the permissions for each role (e.g., Manager vs. Staff).

### E. Daily Close Report & Cash Out ({{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_4}}, {{DATA:SCREEN:SCREEN_2}})
- **Summary:** Aggregate daily totals by payment method (Cash, Card, Points).
- **Cash Reconciliation:** The "Cash Out" modal must allow users to input physical counts of currency.
- **Discrepancy Logic:** The UI must calculate and highlight the difference between "System Totals" and "Manual Counts" in real-time.
